# Creative Commons icons

`cc.svg`, `by.svg`, `nc.svg`, `sa.svg`, `nd.svg` and `zero.svg` are Creative Commons trademarks, downloaded unmodified from
https://mirrors.creativecommons.org/presskit/icons/ on 2026-11-18.

They are used under the Creative Commons Trademark Policy (https://creativecommons.org/policies/), which licenses the button and icon marks
"on the conditions that you use the mark solely to describe the Creative Commons legal tool that applies to a particular work and, in a manner
reasonable to the medium and context, include the URI or hyperlink to the relevant Commons deed on the Creative Commons server."

The generator draws them only next to the licence a drawing is under, in their original colours, with the deed URL printed beside them and linked
in SVG output. Do not edit these files: the policy does not allow modified marks.
